# Assessment writeup

I used Python, BeautifulSoup, Flask and SQLite to separate discovery from approval. The scraper follows every directory page and homepage community link, finding 35 locations: the paginated directory omits Findlay. The matcher compares every CRM account using normalized street and city/state, with exact name and city/state as a fallback for a PO-box record. It avoids misleading same-name facilities in different places. Equivalent address abbreviations are retained. Mixed care offerings are preserved in full.

The review app shows website evidence, all matched candidates, financial checks and field-level changes. Explicit approval is persisted before API writes; rejection persists without mutation. The submitted sandbox was reviewed by Codex (AI), with that identity recorded honestly in the ledger. Of 30 proposals, 29 were approved and applied; the Ashland name-only spelling change was rejected because the existing alias identifies the same facility. Six accounts were created: four missing facilities and two CHOW successors. Seven losing duplicate records were marked Inactive and linked to survivors. Three accounts absent from the complete site were marked Needs Review, retaining ownership rather than guessing a sale. Sandusky's same-address Millstone account warrants further investigation.
